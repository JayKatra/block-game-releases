# Blocklight Privacy Policy

Effective: September 28, 2026

Blocklight is a puzzle game made by Clockless Games. This policy explains what happens to your information when you play it. The short version: nothing leaves your phone.

## What we collect

Nothing. Blocklight does not collect, store or share any personal information.

- No account or sign-in.
- No ads, no analytics and no tracking of any kind.
- The game does not connect to the internet.

## What stays on your phone

Your game in progress, scores, stats and settings are saved only on your own device. We can't see them. Uninstalling the game deletes them.

## Google Play

Blocklight is downloaded through Google Play, which Google runs under its own [privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). Google may give developers general statistics such as install counts and crash reports. These don't identify you, and we use them only to fix problems.

## Purchases

Blocklight currently has no purchases. If optional purchases are added later, payment will be handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your payment details. This policy will be updated before that happens.

## Children

Blocklight isn't aimed at children under 13, and it doesn't collect information from anyone, of any age.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
