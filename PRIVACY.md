# Blocklight Privacy Policy

Effective: October 11, 2026

Blocklight is a puzzle game made by Clockless Games. This policy explains what happens to your information when you play it. The short version: nothing leaves your phone.

## What we collect

Nothing through the game. Blocklight does not collect, store or share any personal information. (The order details Google Play gives us when you buy a theme are covered under Purchases below.)

- No account or sign-in.
- No ads, no analytics and no tracking of any kind.
- The game doesn't send information about you to us or to anyone else. The only connections it makes are to Google Play, on your phone, to show prices, buy themes and bring back themes you've bought.

## What stays on your phone

Your game in progress, scores, stats and settings are saved only on your own device. We can't see them. Uninstalling the game deletes them.

## Google Play

Blocklight is downloaded through Google Play, which Google runs under its own [privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). Google may give developers general statistics such as install counts and crash reports. These don't identify you, and we use them only to fix problems.

## Rating and sharing

Now and then, after a good game, Google Play may show its own "Rate this app" card. Tapping Rate Blocklight in Settings opens the game's Google Play page. Share Blocklight opens your phone's share menu with a short message and a link to the game, and you choose the app it goes to and who receives it. In each case Google Play, or the app you pick, handles it under its own privacy policy. Nothing passes through us.

## Purchases

Blocklight sells optional themes that change how the game looks. Payments are handled entirely by Google Play under Google's own terms and privacy policy. We never see your payment details.

When you buy a theme, Google Play gives us basic order details, such as an order number, the item, the price, the date and your country or region. We use them only to look into problems and refunds you ask us about. The themes you own are remembered on your phone and by Google Play, so they come back on a new phone signed in to the same Google account.

## Children

Blocklight isn't aimed at children under 13, and it doesn't collect information from anyone, of any age.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
